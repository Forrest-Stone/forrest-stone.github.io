To The Supervisor Who Sees This...

Hi, nice to meet you! I want to thank you for taking the time to read through my application and personal statement. My name is Yansen Zhang and I am a third-year Software Engineering graduate student at Sun Yat-sen University. I want to use this opportunity to describe my personal journey in how I got here, my accomplishments, and what I am most passionate about.

How It All Started

My love for programming can be traced back to when I was a high school student. In the reading materials behind the mathematics textbook, I read that writing a program can realize the complicated flow chart. That was the first time I learned about computer. I was very curious about how the computer implemented the calculation, because I often need to check it many times to get the correct answer. This is why I decided to pursue Software Engineering in college. Then I was very excited from "Hello world" to using my own designed algorithm to solve various problems. These projects weren’t crazy complex or anything, but they got me hooked on coding and made me feel proud, happy and satisfied, and that has finalized my decision to further my academic study.

Foundation is Important

I am lucky to have found my passion since college and regarded university professor as my long-term goal. I understand that just learning the required courses is not enough to prepare my career goal, so I took some other courses (i.e. C++, Linux OS, Algorithm Analysis and Design.) to deepen my understanding of professional knowledge. Of course, On my own initiative, I began an aggressive self-education program to teach myself Python, R, Data Analysis, Machine Learning and so on. So I have not only gained a solid knowledge base in mathematics, data analysis and programming, but also sharpened research experience by attending Mathematics Contest in Modeling and other related projects.

I do not like learning knowledge by rote, but like to know the ins and outs. Especially for mathematics courses, I often deduce how formulas come from, and I will summarize methods and think about the connection. One example is, when I summarized the indefinite integral of the form 1/(n+a)*(n+b), I found two methods. One was to transform the formula into the original function arctanh(x), and the other was to find the original function ln(x) by splitting the term. At first, I could not imagine how the graphs of logarithmic function and trigonometric function would be parallel. Later, I was surprised to find "Euler Equation" through derivation independently. The world is so wonderful! In the usual learning life, although some students scored higher in the exam, other students were more willing to come to me and discuss problems, because I can inspire them and share useful learning method with them. Of course, I also follow the same principles in personal tutoring. I will carefully and patiently teach those students to deduce, so that understanding of knowledge will be more solid.

Research is Colorful

Past

There were not many classes in the senior semester, and I had enough time to do research systematically. So I took the initiative to find a more powerful professor Guibing Guo in the college, and then followed him to study the explainable recommendation algorithm. To be honest, it was a bit difficult at first. As a novice, I needed to start from scratch, especially reading English papers. But the passion of learning always motivated me. Under the professor's patient guidance, soon I can read English literature smoothly and exchange my experience and ideas with him. In the end, I also achieved excellent scores in my graduation thesis.

Now

Research on sequence-aware recommendation followed by professor Yubao Liu has been my passion project for the past two years. He has a rigorous attitude towards research and gives us enough time to find problems and exchange ideas. Under the guidance of my supervisor, I published a conference paper as the first author. Yes, Professor Liu had set up this lab and supervised it, and yes other students had helped me hone my skills but, in the end, I had discovered the finding - I found the scientific problems, I did the experiments, I interpreted the results, I wrote the paper, and I realized I could advance knowledge in the field of CS. The experience had honed my skills in thesis writing and spurred me to develop myself scholarly in the future.

For better or for worse, I'm obsessed with learning, and I want to take my pursuit of knowledge to a far greater scale.
