C5: Counterfactual Multi-player Bandits for Explainable Recommendation Diversification

ECML PKDD 2025.

Yansen Zhang, Bowei He, Xiaokun Zhang, Haolun Wu, Zexu Sun, Chen Ma.

We propose a Counterfactual Multi-player Bandits (CMB) method to deliver explainable recommendation diversification across a wide range of diversity metrics. Leveraging a counterfactual framework, our method identifies the factors influencing diversity outcomes. Meanwhile, we adopt multi-player bandits to optimize the counterfactual optimization objective, making it adaptable to both differentiable and non-differentiable diversity metrics.

C4: Shapley Value-driven Data Pruning for Recommender Systems

KDD 2025.

Yansen Zhang, Xiaokun Zhang, Ziqiang Cui, Chen Ma.

We propose Shapley Value-driven Valuation (SVV), a framework that evaluates interactions based on their objective impact on model training rather than subjective intent assumptions. SVV estimates Shapley values in real-time to quantify each interaction’s contribution to reducing training loss, and then highlights high-value interactions while downplaying low-value ones to achieve effective data pruning for recommender systems. This work shifts denoising from heuristic filtering to principled, model-driven interaction valuation.

C3: Decision Information Meets Large Language Models: The Future of Explainable Operations Research

ICLR 2025.

Yansen Zhang, Qingcan Kang, Wing Yin Yu, Hailei Gong, Xiaojin Fu, Xiongwei Han, Tao Zhong, Chen Ma.

We propose a comprehensive framework, Explainable Operations Research (EOR), emphasizing actionable and understandable explanations accompanying optimization. The core of EOR is the concept of Decision Information, which emerges from what-if analysis and focuses on evaluating the impact of complex constraint/parameter changes on decision-making. We utilize bipartite graphs to quantify changes in the OR model and adopt LLMs to improve the explanation capabilities.

J2: Hierarchical Gating Network for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation

ACM TOIS 2025.

Shuliang Wang, Jiabao Zhu, Yi Wang, Chen Ma, Xin Zhao, Yansen Zhang, Ziqiang Yuan, Sijie Ruan.

We propose a Hierarchical Gating Network for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation (HGNCDSR). We simultaneously train single-domain and cross-domain interaction sequences, utilizing a hierarchical gating network to capture user interest representations in single and cross domains. Feature gating and instance gating are applied to extract user interests at the item feature level and instance level.

C2: Learning to Infer User Implicit Preference in Conversational Recommendation

SIGIR 2022.

Chenhao Hu, Shuhua Huang, Yansen Zhang, Yubao Liu.

We propose a new conversational recommender system (CRS) framework called Conversational Recommender with Implicit Feedback (CRIF). CRIF formulates conversational recommendation as a four-phase process: offline representation learning, tracking, decision, and inference. By fully utilizing the relation between users’ attribute-level and item-level feedback, our method can explicitly deduce users’ implicit preferences.

C1: Self-Adaptive Graph Neural Networks for Personalized Sequential Recommendation

ICONIP 2021.

Yansen Zhang, Chenhao Hu, Genan Dai, Weiyang Kong, Yubao Liu.

We propose a self-adaptive graph neural network with future contexts for sequential recommendation (SA-GNN). SA-GNN applies a GNN model with both past and future contexts to model user dynamic interests and utilizes a self-adaptive module and an attention module to weaken the negative effect of unsuitable connections in the item graph.
